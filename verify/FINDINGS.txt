===== FINDING 12 2026-08-22 MAIN@0907825+dirty =====
severity: BLOCKER
core/devblk.cc:128-129 · bounds checks are bypassable by u64 wraparound: `lba+cnt <= nblocks` passes for lba=2^64-1,cnt=1 (sum wraps to 0); `off + cnt*BLK_SECT <= memsz` likewise wraps for near-2^64 off. Consequence: stage = mem+off becomes a wild pointer outside guest linear memory; op=read writes cnt*512 bytes THROUGH the wild pointer (kernel memory corruption from a purely guest-side block request); op=write copies kernel heap contents INTO the RAM disk, readable by other sessions (cross-session info leak). Fix: use __builtin_add_overflow (or range-check each field against limits before composing) for lba+cnt, off, and off+cnt*BLK_SECT; also clamp cnt <= bwMaxSectors-equivalent here since the window protocol allows up to window-size transfers only.
